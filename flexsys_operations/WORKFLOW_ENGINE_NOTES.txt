Operations 19.0.2.4.0 — Workflow Engine Foundation

- Added one central WorkflowService for the order lifecycle.
- Defined explicit allowed transitions for Scheduled, New, Accepted,
  Preparing, Partially Ready, Ready, Completed, Rejected, and Cancelled.
- Routed order actions and automatic line-state synchronization through the
  same transition validator.
- Protected direct model writes from invalid lifecycle jumps while preserving
  idempotent updates and existing event emission.
- Kept terminal states immutable through the standard workflow.
- Added clear business errors that show the permitted next states.
- No new business model or database table was added.
