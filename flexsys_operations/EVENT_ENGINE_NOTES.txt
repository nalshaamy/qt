FlexSys Operations 19.0.2.2.0

Event Engine foundation:
- Added append-only flexsys.operation.event model.
- Added centralized EventService.
- Emits order.created, order.state_changed, and order_line.state_changed.
- Events are company-scoped and optionally branch/POS-scoped.
- Actor is stored as neutral text to support independent FlexSys users.
- No direct application-to-application calls were introduced.
